MICHIGAN FORWARD SURPLUS FIT: S = m*(D/100) + b, Governor 2022 and 2026, all 83 counties
(2026 results UNOFFICIAL / uncertified as of Nov 18, 2026)

DEFINITIONS
S = Democratic governor share of total ballots cast minus D, in percentage points.
D = Democratic "registration" share. Michigan has NO party registration, so D is a stand-in.
  Main run: D = Democratic % of major-party votes in the Aug 2024 U.S. Senate primary, one fixed value per county (your locked instruction).
  Sensitivity: D = Democratic % in that same year's August governor primary (2022 for 2022, 2026 for 2026).
Results come from MI SOS (2022 and 2024 via OpenElections and SOS files). 2026 is unofficial: 27 counties are NBC-scaled estimates (*), Ottawa is from the county canvass (‡), and Muskegon, Ogemaw and Wexford are data-quality counties (†).

RESIDUAL SPREAD (how tight the fit is)
The spread is loose with the Aug-2024 stand-in: residual SD 4.1 to 4.4 pts and IQR about 5 pts. Largest 2026 residuals: Ottawa +14.3, St. Joseph +10.5, Berrien +9.1, Barry +9.0; Luce -8.6, Missaukee -8.0, Ogemaw -7.8.
Counties more than 2 SD from the fit:
  2022: Ottawa, Barry, St. Joseph, Iron
  2026: Ottawa, Berrien, Barry, St. Joseph
These are low-D West Michigan counties, where the Democratic share in November runs well above the Senate-primary share.
With the same-year stand-in, the 2026 fit is tighter (SD 1.9) but explains less (R2 0.33).

SLOPE CHART: CEILING AND FLOOR
Main chart (Aug-2024 D). D is the same number in both years, so each county's shift (S2026 - S2022) is just its 2022-to-2026 Democratic share change. The ceiling counties are therefore simply the biggest Democratic share gainers:
  Berrien +2.65, Kent +2.53, Menominee +2.03, Dickinson +2.00, Ottawa +1.96.
  Shifts across all counties: median -1.16, mean -1.04, range -5.77 (Ogemaw†) to +2.65.
Floor (most negative 2022 S):
  Genesee -7.1 -> -8.3, Iron -6.8 -> -5.2, Houghton -6.8 -> -5.7, Washtenaw -6.5 -> -6.7, Keweenaw -5.7 -> -6.1.
  Under this measure, negative S marks counties whose primary electorate leaned more Democratic than their November vote. That's why Democratic-leaning Washtenaw and Genesee appear here. These are not Republican-baseline counties.
Most Republican baseline (lowest 2022 Democratic share): Missaukee, 26.6%, S +4.3 -> +2.4. It's labeled separately in purple.
  Lowest D (Aug 2024): Barry 12.6, St. Joseph 13.4, Ottawa 14.2, Hillsdale 17.6, Oscoda 18.1.
Same-year chart: every county's shift is negative (median -16.65, range -29.0 to -3.4). That's because the 2022 Democratic primary was uncontested and the 2026 primary was contested, so D jumped from about 46% to about 62% statewide.
  Ceiling = smallest declines: Marquette -3.37, Gogebic -5.31, Iron -6.54, Baraga -6.67, Ontonagon -8.06.
  Floor = most negative 2022 S, excluding counties already in the ceiling: Washtenaw +1.3, Wayne +1.6, Ingham +1.9, Oakland +2.6, Leelanau +2.6.
  Marquette and Gogebic actually have the lowest 2022 S, but they're shown as ceiling.

COMPARISON WITH OTHER STATES
- No true neighbor band exists. Michigan's geographic neighbors (Ohio, Indiana, Wisconsin) also lack party registration.
- No fitted m and b for this equation were saved in the sister-state work on the box. I searched /workspace and /home/box; the regressions there use different setups.
- So I fit the same equation myself on the county files from those analyses, which use actual party registration:

  Band: m -11.3 to +67.6; b -0.9 to +16.4; residual SD 3.3 to 6.9.
- Flag: Michigan's main-run m (-42.4 and -37.8, with CIs entirely below -31) is far outside the band. Its b (+21.6 and +18.8) is above the band too. The 2022 CI is clear of the band, and the 2026 CI just touches its top. Michigan's residual SD of 4.1 to 4.4 is inside the band.
- Why this isn't apples to apples: m = 100*(slope of November share on D - 1). In states with registration, November Democratic share rises faster than Democratic registration, so m is positive. Michigan's primary-based D is more polarized than the November vote (fewer crossover and independent voters), so November share rises only about 0.58 pts per point of D (2022) and m comes out strongly negative. The out-of-band constants reflect the stand-in, not a separate finding about the November vote.
- The same-year 2026 run (m -11.8, b -0.6) lands at the edge of the band, alongside Nebraska 2022 and Iowa 2022 b.

CAVEATS
2026 county results are unofficial, and the NBC-scaled county splits (including Wayne, Oakland and Washtenaw) are estimates. The fit describes how November share relates to a primary-based stand-in. It cannot show injected votes or fraud.
